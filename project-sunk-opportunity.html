Research Project

Sunk Costs vs Opportunity Costs

Amazon · Ford · Tata Motors

Should money already spent influence what a company invests in next? What does a company sacrifice when it chooses one investment over another?

Project Overview

The study applies relevant-cost concepts to three real company cases and examines how past expenditure and alternative uses of resources matter in investment decisions.

Historical estimates are labelled as such. Opportunity costs are not monetarily quantified where public evidence does not establish a value.

Project Contents

Understanding the Concepts

Past expenditure versus the next-best alternative.

SUNK COSTPast expenditure already incurred and not recoverable
OPPORTUNITY COSTBenefit forgone by choosing one option over the next-best alternative
RELEVANT COSTFuture cost or benefit that changes between alternatives

Sunk costs should not, by themselves, influence future investment decisions. Opportunity costs matter because capital, capacity, employee time and management resources committed to one project may no longer be available for another.
